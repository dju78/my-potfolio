case_study:
  problem: "A charity's Board needs to know whether 2025/26 finished on budget, which services and budget lines caused the gap, and what to change for 2026/27."
  contribution: "Designed and built the five-page report, from the data model and measures to the finding-led page titles and the Board recommendations."
  methods: "Budget against actual by month, service and account line; operating position and variance measures; waterfall decomposition of the variance by service; cost per unit and staff hours per unit compared with budget; on-time delivery against target."
  functionality: "Each page answers one Board question: did we finish on budget, what caused the variance, did we deliver what we planned, what does a unit of service cost, and what should the Board do next."
  limitations: "The dataset is synthetic and the charity is fictional. Operating position excludes items not modelled. Cost per unit compares each service with its own budget, because units differ between services. The 2026 spending projection is an illustrative assumption, not a forecast."
