A nine-chapter practical guide for aspiring data analysts, BI professionals and researchers. It follows the analytical workflow from defining KPIs and cleaning data in Power Query to building data models, writing DAX measures and communicating findings.

Worked examples cover retail sales, survey research, budgets and operational performance. The companion hub provides synthetic datasets, guided exercises and answer keys.
