Conferred with the title **Cultural Ambassador of Takete-Ide** on 31 October 2026. This recognition honours my connection to my birthplace in Kogi State, Nigeria, and my commitment to community heritage, cultural preservation and sustainable development. The ceremony coincided with the Takete-Ide centenary celebrations. The presentation is documented by an official certificate and ceremonial sash.

Learn more about the community at [takete-ide.org](https://takete-ide.org/).
